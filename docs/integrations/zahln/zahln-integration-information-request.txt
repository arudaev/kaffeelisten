ZAHLN integration information request

Please return this checklist with every item marked as available, planned,
unavailable, or NDA-required. For anything that is not available now, add the
responsible person and a realistic target date.

Drafts and redacted documents are acceptable. We need enough information to map
your process to Kaffeelisten without making assumptions about your product,
technical interfaces, regulatory model, or responsibilities.

Organisation and current product

- [ ] Confirm the exact product name, correctly spelled canonical domain, legal
      entity, legal form, registered address, registration number, authorised
      signatory, and technical and compliance contacts.

- [ ] Provide the current pitch deck and a dated capability matrix. Mark every
      capability as production, pilot, sandbox, prototype, or planned.

- [ ] Provide a short current-system architecture and data-flow diagram showing
      the frontend, backend, database, hosting, payment components, and external
      providers. Interface-level information is sufficient; source code is not
      requested.

- [ ] Provide the current user flows or screens for individual onboarding,
      company onboarding, QR or deep-link connection, payment, failure,
      cancellation, refund, unlinking, and account deletion.

Regulatory and commercial structure

- [ ] Identify the licensed payment or e-money institution behind every wallet,
      SEPA, payment-initiation, safeguarding, settlement, and payout function.
      Include its authorisation or register reference and describe ZAHLN's
      contractual role.

- [ ] Provide a money-flow diagram naming the customer, merchant or seller,
      payee, merchant of record if applicable, safeguarded and settlement
      accounts, regulated institution, fees, and final payout recipient.

- [ ] Confirm whether ITC1 can contract directly with your legal entity and/or
      regulated institution. State who would own the production merchant account
      and API relationship.

- [ ] Provide the proposed responsibility split for merchant onboarding and KYB,
      user KYC, sanctions and fraud screening, payment authorisation and SCA,
      funds, invoicing, tax records, settlement, refunds, disputes, support,
      incidents, GDPR, retention, and exit.

- [ ] Confirm that Kaffeelisten would not receive funds, payment credentials,
      bank-login data, KYC or KYB documents, selfies, proof-of-funds records, or
      regulatory case files.

Data protection and security

- [ ] Provide the controller, processor, or joint-controller analysis; draft AVV
      or DPA; subprocessor list; hosting and data regions; data categories;
      lawful bases; retention and deletion periods; data-subject process; and
      incident-notification terms.

- [ ] Summarise the relevant security controls: encryption, access control,
      secrets management, audit logs, backup and recovery, vulnerability
      management, penetration testing, incident response, and available audits,
      certifications, or insurance.

Technical integration

- [ ] Provide the versioned API or OpenAPI specification, changelog and versioning
      policy, sandbox base URL, test-account process, and current production-
      readiness status. Do not send real secrets or production credentials.

- [ ] Provide the authentication and account-linking specification, including
      OAuth, OIDC, or the equivalent flow; opaque subject IDs; PKCE and state
      handling; scopes; token lifetime; callbacks; QR and deep links; app-install
      fallback; revocation; and unlinking.

- [ ] Provide the individual and company onboarding specifications, including
      KYC and KYB states, approval and rejection, employee invitations, company
      coverage, limits, revocation, and support ownership.

- [ ] Confirm which Kaffeelisten payment models are supported now: per-purchase
      individual, monthly individual, per-purchase company, and monthly company
      settlement.

- [ ] Provide the API contracts for payment requests, recurring mandates or
      batches, status queries, cancellation, full and partial refunds, reversals,
      disputes, and failed payments. Include the consent and SCA behaviour.

- [ ] Provide the complete payment state model and identify which system and
      state is authoritative for pending, authorised, paid, failed, expired,
      cancelled, refunded, reversed, and disputed payments.

- [ ] Provide the webhook documentation, including event schemas, signatures,
      timestamps and replay protection, key rotation, retries, duplicate or
      out-of-order delivery, event retention, and reconciliation after a missed
      delivery.

- [ ] Provide the idempotency rules, timeout and retry guidance, rate limits,
      maintenance windows, service-level commitments, and canonical
      reconciliation and settlement exports tied to external references.

Invoicing and accounting

- [ ] Provide the proposed invoice and receipt model. Name the legal issuer and
      define VAT treatment, numbering, corrections, delivery, structured
      e-invoice support where applicable, retention, and accounting or DATEV
      export ownership.

Pilot and operating terms

- [ ] Provide the proposed pilot scope: institution or campus, dates, user and
      transaction limits, live versus simulated funds, success criteria, support
      contacts, incident process, rollback, and evidence available from the THA
      pilot.

- [ ] Provide pricing, implementation and API fees, transaction and settlement
      fees, minimum term, limits, SLA and support hours, insurance if applicable,
      termination, data export or return, deletion, and outstanding-payment
      handling.

Confidential and IP-sensitive material

- [ ] For confidential or IP-sensitive information, provide a redacted interface
      or compliance version, or state which material requires an NDA. Proprietary
      source code, algorithms, production secrets, and real customer data are not
      requested.
